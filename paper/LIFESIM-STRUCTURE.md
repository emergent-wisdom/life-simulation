# Life Simulation paper structure

This is the public editorial map for `paper/life-simulation.tex`. It protects the
paper's thesis, asymmetric boundary with the Meaning Model, evidence status,
and claims that are easy to lose during compression. It is not a second
manuscript or a source of scientific evidence.

This map describes the current manuscript. The public release contains its
source, PDF, checks, and selected companion references; development drafts and
historical migration records are excluded. See `docs/RELEASE.md` for the
standalone release workflow.

## Primary job

Life Simulation connects two ambitions: worlds that continue and acquire detail
where interaction needs it, and learners that understand the processes behind
observations. A world may support play, creative work, or exploration before it
becomes a training corpus. Simulation utility and a learning advantage are
separate claims; neither establishes the other. Selected processes carry
versioned histories. A process may
be continuous, discrete-event, piecewise, stochastic, or hybrid. It may be
advanced by direct language-model judgment, ordinary simulation, an estimated
function, or a mixture. The path is required. A compact law is optional and
must beat simpler or direct-model baselines on a declared prospective test.

The abstract leads with the coupled learning system and the objects it can
learn to improve. Constructed worlds provide rich linked learning material;
forward/inverse learning can connect this construction to a growing corpus
model with shared identities and retained disagreement. A process sensorium
predicts the next native text, image, audio, action, or measurement together
with selected explicit process state. Paired world and construction histories
teach temporal behavior and the choice or repair of representations, including
personality models. Later evidence may label an earlier forecast but may never
enter its input. A historical cutoff on a prompt alone cannot exclude later
knowledge in pretrained weights; retrospective reconstruction and clean
prospective testing must be reported separately.

A coequal learning hypothesis makes capability organization itself learnable.
Meaning Model histories of world state and consequences connect to persistent,
concept-defined callable Solvers: Fractal Intelligence tests compositions and
retains, restructures, and reuses capabilities. Learning includes local
specialists, parents, and evaluators as well as internalizing organizational
judgment in one model. Alien search separately revises the categories directing
mechanism exploration. Their histories supply training data because these
organizations act and change; neither contribution reduces to recording traces
or presumes that rationales reveal a mind.

The alignment-facing purpose is to join increasing capability with contextual
understanding of people and an evaluative orientation that affects conduct.
Life Simulation connects nine companion contributions rather than replacing
them. Meaning Model worlds, Life Simulation histories, Understanding Graph,
and Entangled Alignment form the central combination; Imagining the Corpus
and Temporal Hindsight Learning organize synchronized, cutoff-safe training.
Sema keeps definition versions addressable, Fractal Intelligence develops
reusable capability organization, and Ontology of the Alien revises the search
categories directing mechanism exploration. The continuing Reader and its
evaluative orientation are central to the recommended combined training;
accurate models of people do not automatically produce care.
Substrate Language Modeling is a distinct architectural experiment,
not a compulsory dependency of this joint predictor. This is a proposed
solution to alignment, not a demonstrated solution or a substitute for
independent oversight.

Narrative is the first controlled laboratory, not the scope of the method. The
companion Meaning Model paper owns world, concept, and story construction,
including progressive opening, candidate acceptance, story decisions,
rendering, read-back, *The Book of Conditions*, and its matched literary
comparison. Life Simulation can learn from released histories or propose
continuations during construction. Meaning Model rules govern acceptance,
revision, and rendering throughout that interaction; the division is not a
handoff from a finished world or a duplicate construction method.

The shared construction medium is part of the contribution, not merely a
storage arrangement: typed world data, abstract Cuts and concept models,
Event descriptions, actual story text, and externalized understanding are
jointly addressable and revisable without sharing authority. Meaning Model
owns that contract; Life Simulation evolves the processes and constructs
learning views of both their histories and later corrections. Its proposed
data-construction contribution includes expanding sparse annotations into
process-aligned training material across people, processes, timescales,
cutoffs, and synthetic alternatives. Potential volume is not independent
evidence or a demonstrated learning benefit; coverage, cost, and transfer
must also be reported. The full-system comparison concerns the operational
coupling from accepted world through rendering, inference, interpretation,
action, and correction into subsequent learning. Component precedents delimit
mechanisms; they do not substitute for comparing that coupling against
equivalent information and separate training tasks. Neither broad application
scope nor the absence of an identical system establishes originality.

## Claims that must remain visible

1. **One temporal object.** A transition proposes a full interval path and its
   discrete Events; the world interface validates them into accepted history;
   a query selects a projection; and an observation mechanism emits the native
   stream. The selected path is an attachment to the accepted world, never a
   second authority.
2. **Paths before laws.** Recorded paths are constitutive; symbolic, numerical,
   learned, stochastic, and hybrid functions are candidates.
3. **Three roles stay distinct.** Process fields may be categorical; numeric
   coordinates require an independent unit or protocol. Allocations divide one
   unit and sum to one; Events happen and may change several fields but do not
   sum as a set. Shock size is native-unit change, total variation between
   compatible Cuts, or a categorical transition, never a chosen scalar.
4. **Multiscale rhythm.** Slow trajectories, faster shocks, perspective-specific
   anticipation, and later adaptation recur at several scales. Jump diffusion
   is one family, not a law of life.
5. **Perspective is temporal.** An actor's ancestry-derived projection $M_a$, operative
   model $M_a^*$, self-report, plural observer hypotheses, and unknown operative
   organization remain distinct from world history. Authority-bearing parentage
   is separate from temporal nesting and overlap; fitting an interval inside
   another interval does not establish whose assertion it is.
6. **Adaptive resolution is computational.** Unopened state may remain a
   distribution or aggregate, but any fine effect relevant to an active coarse
   prediction must be propagated or opened.
7. **Seven-stage bootstrap.** Construct forward; vary dynamics; learn the
   inverse; project onto licensed material; compile synchronized targets; test
   prospectively; transfer the discipline to other process domains. Generation
   explores candidate mechanisms; failed consequences can motivate different
    dynamics or process/concept distinctions. Correction histories can train a
    successor learner, but independent observations and interventions, not
    narrative coherence, decide whether a candidate explains reality.
    Forward book/game construction supplies examples for inverse learning;
    it does not prove that arbitrary books have uniquely recoverable models.
    Neither direction is declared universally harder. Corpus-wide application
    remains selective, source-grounded, and explicit about unresolved readings.
    Actual-world accounts can contribute to a connected history and geography
    through evidence-supported shared identities, retaining sources,
    disagreements, and access conditions. Fictional and counterfactual worlds
    keep separate contexts. Coverage and local resolution can grow together;
    new evidence may require explicit revision rather than filling every gap.
    Updating this external model is distinct from training weights on selected
    snapshots or construction histories under their own information cutoffs.
8. **Three data regimes.** Simulator-privileged training targets, posterior
   inference from ordinary observations, and explicit sensors or reports at
   deployment are reported separately.
9. **Six epistemic tracks.** Observed, reported, actively elicited, inferred,
   model-completed, and authored records never collapse into one authority.
10. **Two histories, separate clocks.** Model-construction history records how
    an account was proposed, opened, rejected, repaired, and revised.
    Represented-world history records what happens in the modeled world's
    chronological order. Both admit coarse-to-fine learning; neither clock
    substitutes for the other's information cutoff. Endpoint versus history
    is a separate distinction: a finished model can already represent a whole
    life without retaining how that model was built. Separate and combined
    training views use matched training budgets and report information
    differences; paired typed/untyped controls retain the same information.
11. **Process sensorium.** Joint prediction distinguishes native observations
    $Y$, selected process paths $X$, and the discrete Event index $E$ projected
    from accepted history under one cutoff and an explicit process-selection
    query. Neither $X$ nor $E$ is a second authority. It admits unknown,
    unmodeled, uncertain, and version-revision outputs. Definition, schema, and
    profile changes require registry revision; ordinary record instances enter
    through validated world updates under the existing registry. Numerical intuition
    means learning to connect observations to meaningful process quantities,
    not discovering one absolute psychological scale. External units and
    question-relative Cuts retain their different meanings.
12. **Synthetic-centered bootstrap, reality-centered validation.** Authored
    canon is useful ground truth only for the authored world. Under partial
    observation, distinct hidden paths may remain observationally equivalent;
    inversion must preserve uncertainty and score only identifiable state or
    predictive consequences, rather than require unique hidden-state recovery.
13. **Candidate-function discipline.** Candidates advance from observation to
    indicator, held-out association, compact dynamics, cross-environment
    stability, and independent replication. Compression, held-out prediction,
    calibrated scope, and intervention where possible decide whether a law is
    kept; this paper supplies no general discovery procedure.
14. **Integrated alignment hypothesis.** Cognitive annotation is optional for a
    general world model; the continuing Reader and its evaluative orientation
    are central to the integrated alignment profile. EA's full Reader
    treatment starts each generated thought block with the full Reader Core,
    with context-sensitive Refraction affecting the subsequent interpretation.
    Its Understanding Graph persists across a declared source chronology.
    Reader-based learning is intended to inform the Student's later writing,
    dialogue, and action. Life Simulation additionally proposes writer-side
    histories of documented world and scene-construction choices as a further
    training source, distinct from that intended transfer. Tests distinguish
    understanding that changes decisions, causal use rather than recital of
    the orientation, and retained conduct after continued learning or
    successor-like transfer. Neither preserved text nor hashes prove that
    orientation survives, and better prediction does not establish care.
15. **Cross-program uses.** The nine companions retain distinct roles rather
    than becoming aliases for Life Simulation. Imagining the Corpus pairs
    native spans with evolving explanatory visual or schematic tracks; the
    combined profile adds selected process histories and Reader updates.
    Meaning Model and Life Simulation are also a proposed construction method
    for ITC's visuals: images select state, films select Event routes, and
    abstract animations expose relations through examples. The worked unpaid-
    commitment bundle joins exact source, MM records, visual targets, UG
    updates, and Sema references. Visual completion and Reader alternatives
    remain distinct from source evidence; agreement across derived channels
    is not independent corroboration. Joint prediction has an explicit input
    order. The payment example supplies separate forecast-before-notice and
    revise-after-notice input/target rows. A changed explanation preserves
    the earlier nonreceipt fact, attributed belief, and original source;
    generated dependent accounts can be revised or marked stale. These rows
    are illustrative, not an implemented export. Word-like Sema handles
    identify definitions, not guaranteed single
    tokenizer tokens or understanding compressed into hashes.
    THL permits later-informed targets without leaking them into earlier
    inputs. Alien-world work retains target-blind generation separately from
    target-aware transfer and tests lifelike causal consequences, not novelty
    alone. Substrate Language Modeling tests lexical prediction without a
    direct token-history bypass; ITC and the present joint predictor retain
    that route. No compulsory-routing benefit is assumed.
    Music distinguishes song time within a render from production time across
    edits and auditions. Its proposed forward-edit and inverse tasks retain
    alternative explanations, separate listener judgments from measurements,
    and compare aligned histories with audio-only, endpoint, and scrambled
    controls. No audio adapter or corpus is claimed.
16. **Implicit-to-explicit frontier.** The same tasks are compared across
    native-only, content-matched untyped, coarse explicit, adaptive explicit,
    and explicit-heavy conditions. Time-permuted and state-scrambled controls
    isolate temporal alignment from added content; every acquisition,
    validation, maintenance, context, correction, training, and inference cost
    is counted. Evidence for temporal structure requires an incremental
    aligned-minus-scrambled gain under the declared uncertainty and cost rule;
    shared gains from additional content are reported separately.
17. **Observation is not generation.** An observed path can enter the accepted
    world without being attributed to the transition model. Every process fixes
    its address, temporal support, domain or unit, sampling policy, and keeps
    stochasticity, latent-state uncertainty, parameter uncertainty,
    measurement error, heterogeneity, and model discrepancy distinct.
18. **Accumulation is a candidate pattern.** Fast Events may alter a memory or
    resource stock, cross a threshold, move slow state, and thereby change
    later sensitivity, recovery, options, or hazard. It is tested against
    no-memory, linear, and direct-model alternatives rather than asserted.
19. **Revision preserves rivals and scope.** Competing dynamics keep validity
    regions and prospective scores; revision and retirement are versioned and
    scoped rather than retrospective overwrites.
20. **History selection is experimental.** Endpoints, indiscriminate histories,
    selected cutoff-safe invalid edits/repairs/stops, and independently
    corrected on-policy histories form a four-way construction-history
    curriculum. It holds chronological-world supervision fixed while testing
    the selection of construction and correction histories.
21. **Cognitive proprioception is operational.** It means prediction and
    diagnosis from causally relevant task telemetry, not consciousness,
    chain-of-thought access, or a post-hoc rationale.
22. **Reader order is block-causal.** A Reader interpretation is appended only
    after its source interval closes. It may affect later intervals and may
    never rewrite or leak into the earlier source block.
23. **Cuts remain Event-parented and context-complete.** Every Cut is parented by
    an Event and inherits context through declared authority-parent paths to one
    nearest context root. Ambiguous authority is rejected; temporal ancestry is
    identified separately. An evaluative fit Cut belongs to an assessment Event beneath
    the evaluator's declared perspective process and linked about the episode and
    Realization it assesses. Access and cutoff filtering precede query traversal.
    A Cut value is never queried without its permitted parent, question, unit,
    siblings, remainder, constraints, and recomposition rule; if that complete
    context is unavailable, the query is denied without leaking hidden context.
24. **FI has an execution graph.** Run/task episodes contain overlapping
    invocation Events with unweighted dependencies, measured time/token/cost/tool
    data, Concepts for strategies, assessment Events for quality, and derived
    critical path, parallelism, and depth. Solvers are Things in the same world
    as the environment, artifacts, tests, and resources. Executing agents invoke
    those capability interfaces from their own cutoff-safe actor projections.
    Overlapping durations never
    sum, and execution-linked state is not a complete mind. The conflicting
    workshop schedules show why local success can violate a parent's shared
    resource constraint. Learning should include detection and repair of that
    decomposition, not just imitation of the final tree. Later conflict can
    inform a target only to the extent the earlier evidence supported it.
25. **Forecast semantics precede outcomes.** A probability forecast declares its
    outcome space and probabilistic interpretation before the outcome. Empirical
    scoring waits until the outcome is observed. Calibration is a subsequent
    test across forecasts, not the act that makes
    a semantic allocation a probability.
26. **The first benchmark is concrete and proposed.** The two-reservoir example
    specifies its world state, transition and observation mechanisms, cutoff
    input, training targets, evaluation split, baselines, and decision rule.
    Shared-decoder arms separate privileged supervision ($U-N$) from typed
    input organization ($T-U$); the auxiliary state loss is not described as an
    independent joint likelihood. A complete forecast row separates visible
    input from private targets. Mirrored worlds preserve irreducible uncertainty;
    a bounded reopening test compares forecast distributions and returns
    unresolved when retained history is insufficient. These are experiment
    contracts without an implemented benchmark or a measured result.
27. **Decisions and correction.** The human-facing ambition includes choosing
    to act, ask, wait, or decline, then comparing consequences with forecasts
    and revising. Different people's aims and time horizons remain visible;
    understanding their wants does not confer authority to choose for them.
    Practical wisdom is a motivating possibility, not a new primitive or a
    faculty supplied by the grammar. Tests compare equally informed assistants
    and retain consent, refusal, and manipulation risks. The worker-refusal
    example connects emotion to beliefs, wants, relationships, and history;
    competing explanations remain uncertain until evidence or correction
    distinguishes them. Understanding is not privileged access to a mind.
28. **Evaluation has claim-specific prerequisites.** Source integrity, cutoffs,
    applicable replay, and required safety tests cannot be compensated away
    within a use. Optional law discovery and unrelated personal-data transfer
    are not prerequisites for every learning experiment. Failure of an explicit
    track is scoped to the tested training or prediction condition; authoring,
    explanation, and control require their own evidence.

## Paper boundary

### Meaning Model owns

- the record grammar and why its distinctions exist;
- progressive world and concept construction;
- integration of Meaning Model, Understanding Graph, and document surfaces;
- run-specific actor profiles and concept groundings;
- frozen-parent candidate acceptance, revision, and story-selection rolls;
- narrative routing, rendering, read-back, and construction transactions;
- preparation and construction of the new *Book of Conditions*; and
- the matched structured-versus-prose story experiment.

### Life Simulation owns

- continuous, discrete-event, and hybrid process paths;
- transition and observation mechanisms;
- multiscale anticipation, shock, adaptation, and regime change;
- adaptive process resolution as a runtime and statistical problem;
- candidate dynamics and their promotion tests;
- the joint native/process learning objective;
- synthetic-to-inverse-to-real learning and prospective correction;
- separate and combined construction-history and represented-world training,
  endpoint-versus-history comparisons, and Fractal Intelligence trace experiments;
- real-data measurement and governance;
- interactive worlds and the Ontology-of-the-Alien simulation loop; and
- the integrated alignment training proposal and its empirical tests, using
  EA's Reader treatment without redefining it or claiming an identical Writer
  protocol.

### Shared interface

The two papers divide responsibilities that can operate during the same
construction: Meaning Model governs semantic commitments; Life Simulation
proposes and studies temporal behavior, inference, and learning.

Life Simulation may summarize the Meaning Model records it consumes: stable
identities, time-bearing Events, typed world data, permitted authority-parent
paths and separate temporal ancestry, provenance, local Cuts, Realizations,
versioned concept groundings, and aligned native
observations. It may cite the Book as a planned or completed source of process
histories. It must not restate the construction procedure or claim its literary
comparison as a Life Simulation experiment.

## Evidence boundary

The paper develops a method and a prospective experimental program. Its scope
is stated in ordinary prose, with implementation details and evaluation gates
in their respective sections rather than a front-page warning box. The current executor supports immutable
model revisions, versioned world heads, a bounded scalar-law family, and seeded
candidate-state transactions. It does not implement a general hybrid runtime,
adaptive resolution, learned dynamics, joint native/process prediction,
generative inversion, cross-modal recovery, prospective personal modeling, or
alignment training.

The companion Meaning Model evidence package reports the completed Book
manuscript and a partial construction witness. Independent read-back, the
matched story comparison, and the fully aligned process export required here
remain prospective. Its separate synthetic refinement checks do not
retroactively validate those Book properties. No earlier Book/V20 database,
private conversation, archive result, or historical fixture is evidence.

## Editorial guards

- The abstract must define process paths and joint native/process learning,
  while keeping useful continuing worlds visible as a separate ambition and
  naming the integrated alignment proposal without claiming a result.
- The process sensorium and synthetic-to-real loop are central claims, not late
  applications; their defining subsections precede the evaluation program.
- Evaluation precedes adaptive resolution and candidate models, and contains
  the proposed two-reservoir benchmark's world, learner, contrasts, and
  observation limits. Its exact split, training, and scoring protocol belongs
  in the linked appendix, with the complete forecast row.
- Direct language-model continuation precedes the optional candidate-law
  families; narrative construction and the first reservoir learning test are
  distinct starting points, not competing claims to the same first experiment.
- The combined alignment loop precedes its detailed evaluative comparison
  frames. Repeated application summaries may become cross-references without
  deleting their substantive treatment elsewhere.
- The phrase or equivalent of “path required, law optional” must remain.
- Continuous, discrete-event, piecewise, stochastic, and hybrid paths must all
  remain admitted.
- The seven bootstrap stages, six epistemic tracks, three data regimes, four
  function-value tests, six-rung evidential promotion ladder, and
  claim-specific non-compensatory gates must remain visible. The evidential
  promotion of a law is not a universal prerequisite for learning from histories.
- Shock magnitude, recovery half-life, cascade fan-out, shock-to-state delay,
  and state-dependent hazard must remain as operational descriptors rather than
  universal laws.
- The five-level implicit-to-explicit ablation, time-permuted and
  state-scrambled controls, incremental aligned-minus-scrambled decision rule,
  and total-cost accounting must remain.
- Probability semantics are declared before outcomes; empirical calibration
  remains an evaluation question. Semantic allocation alone is not probability.
- Partial-observation inversion retains non-identifiability and scores
  equivalence classes, identifiable quantities, or predictive consequences.
- Music retains its two clocks, intervention task, and inverse alternatives;
  tracks and signal mixtures are not normalized merely because they have parts.
- The decision-and-correction application preserves other people's authority
  over their choices and distinguishes careful failure from reckless success.
- Failure of one explicit-track training condition must not be generalized to
  every authoring, explanation, or control use of the representation.
- Authority-bearing parentage does not follow from temporal nesting. Access and
  cutoff filtering precede query traversal; complete permitted Cut context is
  required, otherwise the query is denied.
- Retrospective reconstructions using pretrained models cannot be called clean
  prospective evidence merely because their prompt stops at a historical cutoff.
- Software, energy, ecology, and scientific simulation examples must keep
  their different evidence standards.
- A known-law control must precede unknown-law discovery claims; rival models
  retain explicit validity regions, revisions, and scoped retirement.
- Observation-only paths must not be attributed to the generator, and the six
  distinct sources of uncertainty must not be pooled.
- Adaptive resolution must preserve anchors and intervention behavior in both
  refinement directions and pass a benefit-minus-total-cost gate.
- Model-construction order and represented-world chronology remain distinct,
